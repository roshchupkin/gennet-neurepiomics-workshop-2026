Two jobs

Training

L1 sparsity makes unused edges go quiet.

  • Remaining weights are attached to named genes and pathways.
  • We will not train UK Biobank in this room.
  • Colab: 1,600 people, 192 SNPs, planted biology, CPU, about 40 seconds.

Interpretation

What you read after training

  • Weight paths — gene and pathway importance.
  • NID — SNP pairs with strong joint weights.
  • DFIM / PathExplain — slower interaction methods in the real CLI.

Attribution is a hypothesis generator, not a diagnosis.

Be honest

Where GenNet should win — and lose

  • Lose: highly polygenic traits, lots of non-coding signal, vs a modern PRS.
  • Win: signal concentrated in annotated genes and pathways.
  • That second case is exactly what the practical plants.
